Privacy Policy

Last updated October 12, 2026

The short version: Klicks doesn't collect, store, or sell your personal information. There's no account, no server of ours, no analytics and no ads. Your car's history and photos stay on your devices, and in your own iCloud if you use it.

What Klicks stores on your iPhone

  • Your cars (a nickname you choose, and optionally the VIN, make, model and year)
  • Your services (oil changes, tire swaps and other car care): dates, odometer readings, notes, and next-service details
  • Photos you take of your odometer, oil change sticker, or receipt
  • Saved recall results (and which ones you've marked as fixed), your winter checklist, and app settings such as your oil change interval

This data is saved on your device using Apple's storage. We don't operate a server, so it is never uploaded to us. Klicks' Home and Lock Screen widgets read the same data on your iPhone to show your next oil change. Using Settings → Erase all Klicks data removes it.

iCloud sync

If you're signed in to iCloud, Klicks keeps a copy of your data in your private iCloud database using Apple's CloudKit, so it's backed up and appears on your other devices. This data belongs to your Apple Account: we can't see or access it. You can turn this off for Klicks in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud.

Reading your photos

Klicks reads the numbers in your odometer, sticker, and VIN photos using Apple's Vision framework, entirely on your iPhone. Photos are never uploaded.

Recall checks

If you add your VIN, Klicks sends it to the U.S. National Highway Traffic Safety Administration's public vPIC service, once, to look up your car's make, model and year. To check recalls, Klicks sends only the make, model and year (not your VIN) to Transport Canada's public Vehicle Recalls Database and, if that's unavailable or has no match, to NHTSA's public recall service. These are public government lookups. They don't require an account, and we receive nothing from them except the results shown in the app. Klicks re-checks about once a week in the background so it can tell you about new recalls.

Location

Klicks asks for your approximate location only when you use a feature that needs it: checking the local forecast for tire season, or finding shops and dealers nearby. Your location is passed to Apple's WeatherKit and Apple Maps to answer that request. Klicks doesn't store or track your location, and it never uses location in the background.

Notifications

Reminders (oil changes, tire swaps, car care, new recalls) are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. Actions like "Log it" or "Remind me next week" are handled on your device. iCloud may use Apple's silent push service to keep your data in sync; no other push service is involved.

Sharing a PDF

If you choose Share PDF, Klicks creates a service-history document on your iPhone and hands it to the app you pick (Mail, Messages, Files…). What happens next follows that app's privacy practices.

What Klicks doesn't do

  • No account or sign-in of any kind
  • No analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No selling or sharing of data, because there's no data to sell

Children's privacy

Klicks is not directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, because it doesn't collect information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
