How does Klicks know when my oil change is due?

It uses your last oil change and the usual interval (8,000 km or 6 months, whichever comes first, and you can change this in Settings). If you added a photo of the shop's windshield sticker, it uses the sticker's km and date instead. After two odometer readings, Klicks learns how much you actually drive.

It read the wrong number from my odometer photo.

Just tap the number on the confirm screen and fix it with the keypad. Photos with the total km clearly in frame (not the trip meter) work best.

Do I have to keep updating my km?

No. It's optional, but a quick "Update km" now and then makes the estimate sharper.

Where do recall results come from?

From Transport Canada's official Vehicle Recalls Database. If it can't be reached or has no match for your model, Klicks shows U.S. recalls from NHTSA and labels them clearly. Your dealer can confirm whether a recall applies to your exact car, and recall repairs are free.

Why does it want my location?

Only for two optional things: the local forecast for tire season, and finding shops nearby. It's never stored or tracked.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

Klicks keeps everything on your iPhone. Your data comes along when you restore your new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup of your old one.

How do I delete my data?

Open Klicks → Settings → Erase all Klicks data, or delete the app.
